Everything a small team needs to think on one canvas

Built for classes, study groups, interviews and quick design reviews.

Live drawing

Pen strokes stream to everyone as you draw, with adjustable colour and brush size.

Shapes, arrows and text

Add lines, arrows, rectangles, ellipses and labels to turn a sketch into a diagram.

Room chat

Talk next to the board. See who is typing and catch up on earlier messages when you join.

Live cursors

Every person gets a colour and a name tag, so you always know who is pointing at what.

Undo and redo

Each person undoes only their own work, so nobody removes a teammate's drawing by accident.

Save as an image

Download the finished board as a PNG, or share the room link so others can join in.

Start in three steps

  1. Create a room

    Enter your name and press the button. You get a six-character room code.

  2. Send the link

    Copy the invite link from the top bar and share it with your group.

  3. Draw and chat

    Everyone who opens the link lands on the same board, in real time.
